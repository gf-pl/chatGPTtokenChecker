What the numbers mean

The percentage is remaining capacity in each plan window. The time shown is the next reset of that window. Available resets are credits you can choose to redeem in Codex; this dashboard never redeems them. The source reports balances and available resets, but does not provide your plan price, automatic top-up setting, or a complete history of past reset redemptions.

Card charge and invoice dates are not provided by the connected account data. For a self-service monthly ChatGPT subscription, billing repeats on the day you subscribed, and invoice dates follow that schedule. See the OpenAI billing guidance.

Each account has its own sign-in stored in the Docker data volume. This page is bound to your computer by default.

Connect each ChatGPT account once with the official Codex sign-in. This monitor reads plan usage automatically every few minutes and keeps a local history. Your OpenAI API Admin keys are not used.
